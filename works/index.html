Mandate.

Featured

Mandate is a financial control layer that helps people understand and manage automated financial decisions before they happen. It brings AI-agent permissions, lender decisions and financing offers into one secure place, making every action visible, explainable and controllable.

Instead of vague rejection messages, Mandate explains the factors behind a decision and lets people create plain-language rules such as “Pay recurring bills under ₹5,000 without asking me.”
