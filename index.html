About me

a little about myself

My journey into design started with a love for visual creativity. I began by exploring graphic design, branding and logo design before moving into UI/UX and product design. Today I focus on designing intuitive digital products, websites and visual identities that balance user needs, business goals and visual detail.

I enjoy continuously learning, experimenting and building products that improve my craft as a Product Designer.

I shape thoughtful digital products for tangled workflows, build clear visual systems and care about the details people actually feel.

Design process

Journey from idea to impact

A thoughtful path from understanding the real problem to shaping, testing and delivering a product people can use with confidence.

01

Stage 01

Research & Synthesis

Understand the problem before designing.

I study users, business goals and the wider product context, then organise the findings into clear insights and opportunities.

02

Stage 02

Prioritise & Define

Decide what matters most.

I align user needs with business priorities, define the product direction and create focused user flows before moving into design.

03

Stage 03

Design & Prototype

Turn direction into an experience.

I create wireframes, interface systems and interactive prototypes to explore ideas and make the experience clear, usable and consistent.

04

Stage 04

Test & Iterate

Validate before finalising.

I test ideas with users and stakeholders, identify friction and refine the design using real feedback.

05

Stage 05

Deliver & Measure

Support the product beyond the design file.

I collaborate with developers, review implementation and use feedback and product outcomes to improve the experience after launch.
